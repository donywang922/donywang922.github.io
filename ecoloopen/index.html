Feasibility & rigour

We killed our own
electric version.

Evidence in. Electric out.

  1. 01Flooded category
  2. 02Water + electronics
  3. 03Steel shields the field
Rinse, not sterilize 15s by hand 2h insulation

2:35–3:07

We did not start here. Our first version was electric. Then a mechanical engineer challenged it: electric mixers already crowd the market; water and electronics create a durability risk; and a steel base can shield the magnetic field. He was right. We killed that version and redesigned around hand power. We also own the trade-offs: EcoLoop rinses, it does not sterilize; the user twists for fifteen seconds; and insulation is an honest two hours, not all day.
